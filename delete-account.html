Account deletion

Ultimate Tic Tac Toe: Conquest

You can delete your Conquest account in the app or request deletion by email if you cannot access it.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to the Google account used for Conquest.
  2. Open your profile screen and choose the account deletion option.
  3. Confirm the request and complete the Google identity check.

Request deletion by email

Email us from the Google account address used to sign in. Include “Account deletion request” in the subject and your Conquest display name in the message so we can identify the account. Do not include your password or other sign-in credentials. If you contact us from a different email address, we may ask for additional information to verify that you control the account.

Email an account-deletion request

We will review the request and may contact you to verify account ownership. Do not send your Google password.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account and player profile.
  • Your personal match history and leaderboard entry.
  • Leaderboard events associated with your account.
  • Unfinished rooms involving your account are ended.

Completed shared match records may remain for the other players. In those records, your display name is replaced with “Deleted player” and your move records use a room-specific anonymous identifier. Other players’ match histories are preserved. Therefore, deleting your account does not erase every shared match record.

Account deletion does not delete your Google account or information Google processes independently for Google services or advertising. Use Google’s privacy and advertising settings to manage that information.

See the Privacy Policy for more information about how Conquest handles data.
