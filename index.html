Privacy Policy

Ultimate Tic Tac Toe: Conquest

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how Ultimate Tic Tac Toe: Conquest (“Conquest,” “we,” or “the app”) handles information when you play. Conquest is operated by its publisher. For questions or requests, contact innovitechoo@gmail.com.

Information we handle

  • Sign-in information: Conquest uses Google Sign-In with Firebase Authentication. Google and Firebase process account identifiers and basic Google account information, such as your name and email address, to authenticate you. Your email is not shown to other players as your public game name.
  • Player profile: Conquest stores your Firebase user identifier, chosen display name, avatar selection, and profile timestamps in Firebase. Your display name and avatar may be visible to other players.
  • Gameplay and online services: Online rooms use player seats, readiness and room status, board moves, match results, and related timestamps to operate multiplayer matches. Match results contribute to leaderboard statistics and personal match history. Other players can see information needed to play, including your display name, team or seat, game state, and results. Leaderboard entries are visible to users of the online leaderboard.
  • Service and security data: Firebase and Google may process technical request, security, and service-operation information when providing authentication, database, and server functions.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • sign you in and maintain your player profile;
  • create and run online rooms, validate moves, and synchronize matches;
  • show match history and calculate leaderboard results;
  • protect, maintain, and troubleshoot the game and its online services;
  • respond to support questions and account-deletion requests.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide the game:

  • Google and Firebase: Google provides sign-in and Firebase services used for authentication, online data storage, and server-side game operations. Those services process information under Google's terms and privacy practices.
  • Other players: Your chosen display name, avatar, seat/team, online game state, and match results are shared where necessary for multiplayer and leaderboard features.
  • Legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or the public.

Conquest does not use personal information for targeted advertising. Google’s privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase privacy information at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Retention and account deletion

We retain account and gameplay information while it is needed to provide the service. You can request deletion in Conquest from your profile screen. The app asks you to confirm your Google identity before processing the request.

Deletion removes your Firebase account, profile, personal match history, leaderboard entry, and related leaderboard events. Unfinished rooms involving your account are ended. Completed shared match records may be retained for the other players; your display name is replaced with “Deleted player” and your move records use a room-specific anonymous identifier. This means shared match records are not necessarily erased when your account is deleted.

If you cannot access the app, use the account deletion request page to contact us. We may need information sufficient to verify that you control the account. Never send your Google password.

Security and international processing

We and our service providers use safeguards intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Google and Firebase may process information in countries where their systems or service providers operate, which may be outside your country.

Children’s privacy

Conquest is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. We will update the effective date on this page when we publish a revised version.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email innovitechoo@gmail.com.
